All games compatible with the Circle Pad Pro are natively supported by the C-Stick and the additional shoulder triggers (ZR and ZL) on the New 3DS/2DS (XL).

:::info
Some games are compatible only with the New Nintendo 3DS C-Stick, ZR and ZL buttons, and do not support the Circle Pad Pro. They are listed here: **[C-Stick, ZR and ZL Compatible Games on new 3DS](/docs/compatibility/only-new-3ds/cstick-games)**.
:::

#### Relevance is rated using the following criteria (/4): 

- **T (Triggers)**: Meaningful use of ZL/ZR buttons *<small>(e.g. ❌ Zelda MM3D - triggers simply duplicate L/R)</small>*
- **S (Stick)**: Proper use of the right stick *<small>(e.g. ❌ Shantae Pirate's Curse - right stick is only used for inventory navigation)</small>*
- **F (Feature)**: Adds new functionality not available without the CPP *<small>(e.g. ✅ Zelda MM3D - free camera control is not available without CPP | ❌ MH4U -  free camera control is already available with dpad)</small>*
- **C (Controls)**: Significantly improves gameplay and is essential for the optimal experience *<small>(e.g. ✅ RE Revelations - CPP is a game changer | ❌ FS18 - no significant gameplay benefit)</small>*
