Here are games that are not compatible with the Circle Pad Pro, but are only compatible with the C-Stick and/or the ZR and/or ZL buttons on New models (New 3DS / New 3DS XL / New 2DS XL).

:::info
All games compatible with the Circle Pad Pro are also natively supported by the C-Stick and the additional shoulder triggers (ZR and ZL) on the New 3DS/2DS (XL). They are listed here: **[Circle Pad Pro Compatible Games](/docs/compatibility/circle-pad-pro/circle-pad-pro-games)**.
:::

This is mainly due to developers overlooking the Circle Pad Pro, often being unaware of its existence, compared to the New 3DS. It is also related to the hardware limitations of older models. Some games were designed specifically for the New 3DS *(e.g. Xenoblade Chronicles 3D)*, and the Circle Pad Pro requires around [5% extra CPU usage](https://mettrelien) (because of infared communication), which could be unsustainable in more demanding games due to performance drops *(e.g. Super Smash Bros)*.

#### Relevance is rated using the following criteria (/4): 

- **T (Triggers)**: Meaningful use of ZL/ZR buttons *<small>(e.g. ❌ Zelda MM3D - triggers simply duplicate L/R)</small>*
- **S (Stick)**: Proper use of the right stick *<small>(e.g. ❌ Shantae Pirate's Curse - right stick is only used for inventory navigation)</small>*
- **F (Feature)**: Controls adds new functionality not available on old 3DS *<small>(e.g. ✅ Zelda MM3D - free camera control is not available on old 3ds without CPP | ❌ MH4U -  free camera control is already available with dpad)</small>*
- **C (Controls)**: Significantly improves gameplay and is essential for the optimal experience *<small>(e.g. ✅ RE Revelations - CPP is a game changer | ❌ FS18 - no significant gameplay benefit)</small>*
